# Executive Summary

The NexaChain Intelligence Platform is an enterprise-scale analytics and machine learning initiative designed to simulate a real-world consulting engagement involving supply chain operations, procurement intelligence, logistics performance, vendor risk management, inventory optimization, and financial risk analytics.

The project is based on a fictional multinational organization, **GlobeNex Manufacturing & Logistics Corporation**, operating across 47 countries with a highly complex ecosystem of suppliers, warehouses, transportation partners, procurement teams, and customers.

The primary objective of this engagement is to transform large-scale operational data into actionable business intelligence through analytics, forecasting, machine learning, and executive reporting.

This repository serves as the central collaboration platform for all Data Analyst, Data Scientist, and Machine Learning Engineer teams participating in the project.

# Business Challenges

GlobeNex currently faces several strategic and operational challenges impacting profitability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

---

## Vendor Risk Management

The organization depends on a large network of suppliers to support manufacturing and distribution activities.

Current challenges include:

- Supplier dependency risks
- Vendor performance inconsistencies
- Procurement disruptions
- Lack of proactive vendor risk monitoring
- Increased operational exposure

### Business Question

Which suppliers represent the highest operational and financial risk?

---

## Inventory Optimization

The company experiences both inventory shortages and excessive stock accumulation.

Current challenges include:

- Overstocking
- Stockouts
- High carrying costs
- Inefficient replenishment planning
- Working capital inefficiencies

### Business Question

How can inventory be optimized while maintaining service levels?

---

## Logistics Performance

Transportation operations continue to experience delivery delays and cost escalations.

Current challenges include:

- Route inefficiencies
- Weather disruptions
- Carrier performance variability
- Increasing transportation costs

### Business Question

Can delivery delays be predicted before they occur?

---

## Financial Risk Analytics

Operational decisions directly impact financial outcomes.

Current challenges include:

- Cash flow uncertainty
- Working capital constraints
- Procurement cost escalation
- Revenue leakage

### Business Question

How can financial exposure be monitored and forecasted proactively?
